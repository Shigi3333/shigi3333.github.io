description: Stairwell was a student-teacher collaboration platform. Our scrappy team of 3, guided by our Software Startups professor, developed it in order to fill a gap we had seen forming between how students and teachers interacted through online assignments. We wanted to ensure that professors had a strong oversight of their students' learning from an assignment. We also wanted to provide a judgement-free means where students could provide feedback on assignments, and explain how what they had done impacted their learning. This gave professors who used our tool a much deeper understanding of how the design of their courses was fostering the understanding their students were gaining. We were able to bring our platform to a total of 4 courses between Mines and CU Boulder, garnering about 300 total users.
