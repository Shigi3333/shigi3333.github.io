description: As part of a sponsored internship with Lunar Outpost, I worked on a team of 4 to develop an visual colocalization pipeline for their rovers. The goal of the pipeline was to be able to recognize when another rover of the same model was in frame of it's onboard camera, and extrapolate it's relative positional and rotational information. As an educational exercise, we were also encouraged heavily to practice Agile project management techniques. Using  the management principles taught in class, we were able to develop something that exceeded our clients expectations, and was able to represent the orientation of a simplified object in space detected by our program. At the close of the semester, the program, and documentation about each part, was left with the client to ensure it would be something they could adapt into their existing onboard systems.
